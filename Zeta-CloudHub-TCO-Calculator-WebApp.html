The business case

Why Zeta CloudHub beats building a private cloud

1 · Zero CAPEX, immediate value

A private cloud demands a six-figure upfront investment — spent before the first workload ever runs. Zeta CloudHub converts this into a predictable monthly OPEX that starts small and scales with actual usage.

2 · Pay only for what you use

Private clouds are sized for peak demand plus N+1 redundancy, so 40–60% of purchased capacity typically sits idle. On Zeta CloudHub you provision exactly what you need today and grow — or shrink — instantly.

3 · 24×7 expert operations included

Managed OS, databases, firewalls, backup and security are delivered by certified engineers as part of the service. Replicating this in-house means hiring and retaining a round-the-clock operations team.

4 · No refresh cycle, no obsolescence

On-prem hardware must be refreshed every 4–5 years, repeating the CAPEX shock, while warranties expire even earlier. The Zeta CloudHub platform is continuously upgraded at no extra cost.

5 · Resilience & local data residency

Data stays within Pakistan on an enterprise-grade platform with built-in redundancy, backup and DR options — meeting regulatory and data-sovereignty expectations out of the box.

6 · Speed to market

A private cloud takes 3–6 months to procure, build and stabilize. Zeta CloudHub workloads can be provisioned in hours, letting the business capture opportunities instead of managing infrastructure projects.

7 · Financial clarity in an inflationary market

Local power, real estate and staffing costs escalate faster than cloud pricing. Discounted Zeta CloudHub rates with a fixed price book give CFOs a clean, defensible cost line.
